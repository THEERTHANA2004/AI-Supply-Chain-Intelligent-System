# Dashboard Specification

## Purpose

The dashboard provides an overall view of supply-chain conditions,
AI predictions, operational risks and decision-support insights.

## Header

- Application Name: AI Supply Chain Intelligence
- Logged-in User
- User Role
- Notification Indicator
- Settings

## Summary Cards

### 1. Demand Forecast
Displays predicted demand and demand trend.

### 2. Supplier Risk
Displays overall supplier risk level.

### 3. Inventory Status
Displays current inventory health and stock alerts.

### 4. Workforce Status
Displays workforce availability and shortage status.

### 5. Supply Chain Risk
Displays overall calculated supply-chain risk.

## Main Visualizations

### Demand Forecast Chart

Displays:
- Historical demand
- Predicted demand
- Demand trend

### Supplier Risk Overview

Displays:
- Supplier risk levels
- Supplier ranking
- High-risk suppliers

### Inventory Overview

Displays:
- Current stock
- Safety stock
- Reorder point
- Stock-out alerts

### Risk Propagation Graph

Displays:
- Supply-chain entities
- Dependencies
- Risk propagation
- High-risk nodes

## AI Insights

The dashboard displays automatically generated insights based on
structured results from the AI modules.

Examples:

- Demand is expected to increase.
- A supplier has elevated delivery risk.
- Inventory may fall below the reorder point.
- Workforce shortage may affect operations.
